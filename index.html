Endometriosis Solution
Research prototype

Early access list

An AI that finds endometriosis lesions in surgery video, and shows its working.

Superficial peritoneal endometriosis is small, subtle and easy to miss at laparoscopy. We are building an explainable deep learning tool that flags it and highlights the region it looked at, so clinicians can check the reasoning instead of trusting a score.

Who the waitlist is for

Endometriosis takes seven to ten years to diagnose on average. The people who can shorten that include these four groups.

Biotech and pharma

Imaging biomarkers for trials, and lesion detection that can be audited.

You get: briefings on the method, validation and collaboration options.

Insurers and payers

Earlier, better documented diagnosis may reduce years of repeat visits and tests.

You get: plain-language summaries of what the evidence does and does not show.

Students and educators

A worked example of explainable AI on real surgical images, with open methods.

You get: teaching-friendly explainers and the demo interface.

Curious readers

Want to understand how a model can point at what it saw, and where it still fails?

You get: occasional short updates, written for non-specialists.

What we are building

Detect

A classifier for subtle lesions

A general model screens frames for endometriosis, and a second model is specialised for the superficial peritoneal form, which is the hardest to see.

Explain

Heatmaps you can check

Every prediction comes with an attention map, scored against expert-drawn lesion outlines so we can tell when the explanation is poor.

Try

A browser demo

Upload a frame, see the prediction, the confidence and the overlay in seconds. Waitlist members get first access.
